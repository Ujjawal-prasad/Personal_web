Selected Robotics Projects

Quality-focused project stories with my role and technical stack.

Martian Terrain Mapping with Aerial Intelligence

Problem: Build a quadcopter pipeline for autonomous surface mapping and landing-site assessment in an ISRO competition context.

Approach: Integrated Intel RealSense D435i, Jetson Orin Nano, Pixhawk 6C, and ARK Flow optical flow. Built ROS 2 Humble + RTAB-Map VIO/SLAM stack with MAVLink and CAN communication.

My Contribution: Perception and localization pipeline design, sensor fusion flow, and mapping workflow integration.

Stack: ROS 2 Humble, RTAB-Map, librealsense2, MAVLink, Pixhawk, Jetson Orin Nano.

Autonomous Exploration in GPS-Denied Environments

Problem: Enable UAV exploration in unknown environments without GPS, such as collapsed structures.

Approach: Used LiDAR-based perception in Gazebo, point cloud + visual odometry, RTAB-Map loop closure, occupancy grid conversion, BFS frontier detection, and Nav2 behavior-tree navigation with recovery actions.

My Contribution: Exploration logic integration from map generation to frontier goal selection and autonomous execution flow.

Stack: ROS 2, Nav2, RTAB-Map, Gazebo, ICP, LiDAR PointCloud2/LaserScan.

SKYBOT Series (SKYBOT 1 to 3)

Problem: Build stable and extensible custom aerial robots from scratch through iterative prototyping.

Approach: Progressed from Arduino-only attempts to Pixhawk closed-loop control, diagnosed instability, then redesigned around hardware-control compatibility and later integrated Jetson Orin Nano + RealSense D435i for autonomous mission readiness.

Key Lessons: Matching motor KV and propeller torque demand is critical. Crash behavior and frame stress must drive component choices.

Outcome: Stable SKYBOT2 flight and SKYBOT3 under development for payload-focused missions.

Autonomous Ground Rover for Search and Rescue

Problem: Reduce risk to rescue teams in dangerous environments such as earthquake and mine-collapse zones.

Approach: Built autonomous mapping rover architecture with SLAM and integrated mmWave sensing to detect human vital-sign micro-movements under debris.

My Contribution: Solely handled software architecture, mapping stack design, and integration of RealSense, Jetson, Pixhawk, motor drivers (Modbus), and Arduino communication (UART).

Stack: ROS 2 Humble, RTAB-Map, RealSense D435i, Jetson Orin Nano, Pixhawk 2.4.8, MAVLink.

Terrain-Adaptive Autonomous Weed Removal Robot

Problem: Improve agricultural weed removal with a terrain-adaptive autonomous system.

Approach: Worked on perception by fine-tuning YOLOv8 on a Kaggle weed/crop dataset for object detection and bounding-box based targeting.

My Contribution: Perception pipeline, model fine-tuning, and evaluation on test data.

Stack: YOLOv8, dataset preparation, detection evaluation.
